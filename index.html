Working paper and research in progress

A subnational geocoded dataset of global natural-hazard disasters in EM-DAT, 2000–2025

Data note with Florent Bresson (CERDI), 2026 · Manuscript available on request

  • Geocoded 9,893 EM-DAT natural-hazard events into 49,922 sub-national administrative polygons across 195 countries, resolving locations to the fourth administrative level.
  • Built a priority-ordered matching cascade (GAUL/GADM codes, harmonised gazetteer names, then OpenStreetMap and Photon), flagging the provenance of every location; 86.4% resolved from structured codes.

Road Infrastructure and Armed Conflict: Evidence from Ethiopia's Road Sector Development Program

In progress

  • Assembled a balanced 1997–2024 panel of 780 woredas and 15,714 kebeles from road-network, ACLED, satellite and climate sources.
  • Computed market-access indices from gravity models with cost-distance travel-time matrices, capturing trade costs and market integration.
  • Designed an identification strategy combining continuous-dose TWFE and a long-difference IV from a minimum spanning tree.
